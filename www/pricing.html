Questions

Is employee tracking always on?

No. Location is collected only between Start Duty and End Duty, during configured working hours, and employees always see when tracking is active. This supports consent and transparency under India's DPDP Act.

Do I need GPS hardware?

No. The app uses the GPS in the employee's Android phone.

Can we customise visit forms and workflows?

Yes. Visit types, outcomes, form fields, geofence radius, working hours and payment modes are all configurable from the admin console.
